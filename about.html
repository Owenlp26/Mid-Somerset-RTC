Who We Are

About the Academy

Our story, our values, and the people who make Midsomerset RTC the club it is today.

Our Story

Born from a Passion for Player Development

Midsomerset RTC was established with a clear and simple purpose: to give talented young footballers in Somerset the opportunity and environment they deserve. As one of the Football Association's officially registered Regional Talent Clubs, we are part of a national network designed to bridge the gap between grassroots football and the professional academy system.

Our club brings together experienced, FA-qualified coaches who share a genuine passion for youth development. From our very first session, the ethos has been player-centred — every decision we make is driven by what is best for the young people in our programme. We believe that with the right coaching, the right environment, and the right values, any talented player can progress.

Rooted in the Somerset community, we work closely with local clubs, schools, and County FA partners to identify players and create opportunities. We are proud of where we come from and proud of the players we have helped develop — players who have gone on to professional academies, county squads, and beyond.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

Three core commitments that underpin everything we do at Midsomerset RTC.

🎯

Develop Talent

We provide a high-quality, structured coaching environment that challenges players to improve their technical skills, tactical understanding, and physical conditioning. Every session is purposefully designed with individual development in mind.

⭐

Build Character

We believe football is a vehicle for life lessons. We instil values of respect, resilience, discipline, and teamwork — qualities that serve players both on the pitch and in all areas of life. We develop people as much as we develop players.

🚀

Create Pathways

We actively create and maintain clear progression routes for our players — including liaison with professional club academies, county representative squads, and further education performance programmes. Your journey starts here.

The People Behind the Club

Coaching Staff

Our coaches are experienced, FA-qualified professionals who are passionate about developing the next generation of Somerset footballers.

JM

James Mitchell

Head Coach

James holds his UEFA B Licence and has over 12 years of experience in youth football development. He oversees the entire RTC programme, ensuring our curriculum meets the highest FA standards.

SK

Sarah Knowles

Academy Coach

Sarah is an FA Level 2 qualified coach specialising in player development for the U8–U12 age groups. Her sessions combine creativity with structured technical work, making development enjoyable for every player.

RP

Ryan Pearce

Goalkeeper Coach

Ryan is a dedicated goalkeeper coach with experience across multiple professional youth setups. He works with shot-stoppers across all age groups, providing specialist coaching that is often hard to find at youth level.
