About the Academy

Our story, our values, and the people who make Mid-Somerset JPL Talent Centre the club it is today.

Our Story

Built on a Passion for Player Development

Mid-Somerset JPL Talent Centre was established with a clear and simple purpose: to give talented young footballers in Somerset the environment and pathway they deserve. As part of the official Junior Premier League (JPL) pathway, we are connected to a national network designed to develop players through structured, quality football.

We are proud to be JPL National Champions 2018 — the highest honour in the Junior Premier League — a milestone that reflects the talent of our players and the dedication of our coaching staff. That winning culture runs through everything we do.

Rooted in the Somerset community, we run sessions across three venues: Strode College Sports Centre in Street, Writhlington Sports Centre in Radstock, and St Mark's School in Bath. We cater for Boys (U7–U13) and Girls (U8–U15) through our dedicated Girls Centre of Excellence, with goalkeeper training available at all venues.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

Three core commitments that underpin everything we do at Mid-Somerset JPL Talent Centre.

Develop Talent

We provide a high-quality, structured coaching environment that challenges players to improve their technical skills, tactical understanding, and physical conditioning through the JPL development framework.

Build Character

We believe football is a vehicle for life lessons. We instil values of respect, resilience, discipline, and teamwork — qualities that serve players both on the pitch and in all areas of life.

Create Pathways

We actively create and maintain clear progression routes for our players through the JPL structure, including county representative squads and beyond. Your journey starts here.

The People Behind the Club

Coaching Staff

Head Coach

Boys Programme

Our head coach oversees the Boys programme from U7 to U13, delivering a structured JPL curriculum that challenges every player to reach their potential, week on week.

Girls Head Coach

Girls Centre of Excellence

Leading our Girls Centre of Excellence across U8–U15, our girls head coach provides a specialist, high-quality JPL development environment for female players in Somerset.

Goalkeeper Coach

All Venues · All Age Groups

Dedicated specialist goalkeeper coaching available at all three training venues. Our goalkeeper coach works with shot-stoppers across all age groups in both the Boys and Girls programmes.
