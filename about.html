Who We Are

About Mid-Somerset RTC

A football academy built on passion, purpose, and a genuine belief that every talented child deserves a chance.

Our Mission

Developing Boys and Girls Into the Next Generation of Footballers

Mid-Somerset RTC exists to give young players across Somerset the environment they need to grow, as footballers and as people. We provide academy-level coaching, high-level competition, and genuine exposure to professional pathways, all while making sure every child still has a genuine love for the game.

Through the Junior Premier League pathway, we bridge the gap between grassroots football and elite academy environments, giving talented boys and girls access to life changing opportunities. Every session is built on the same standards used by professional clubs, delivered by coaches who are genuinely invested in seeing every child succeed, both on the pitch and off it.

Our Story

Where It All Began

Founder

Paul Taylor

Founder & Academy Director · JPL Allstars National Lead

Paul is the owner and founder of Mid-Somerset RTC, and football has been his life since he was five or six years old, kicking a ball around in the local parks. He was fortunate enough to play academy football as a youngster, and that early taste for the game set him on a path into coaching. Over a 25-year career in professional and academy football, he's worked for the Football Association, Bristol City, and other academies at senior and head coach level, learning from some of the best in the game.

Eight years ago, Paul founded Mid-Somerset RTC because he could see there were genuinely talented footballers all across this part of the world who weren't getting the environment they deserved. He wanted to build that environment himself: one where the best young players could be challenged, developed, and made better. Alongside running the academy, he now serves as National Lead for the JPL All Stars programme across London and the Southwest.

But what drives him hasn't changed since he was that kid in the park. It's seeing young people thrive, develop, and fall in love with the game the way he did. Fifty years on, he's still here, still coaching, and still loving every single minute of it.

The Team

Our Coaching Staff

Experienced, qualified, and passionate about developing the next generation.

Foundation Phase

Zach

Head of U7–U11 Foundation Phase · Assistant Head of Coaching

Having come through the professional academy system himself before being released at 16, Zach understands both the opportunities and challenges that football can bring. That experience inspired him to give back, and he's now spent over 13 years coaching young players.

He's passionate about helping every child reach their potential, whether supporting those with ambitions of progressing through the academy pathway or helping them build confidence, become active, and fall in love with the game. He's committed to creating an environment where players feel comfortable to learn, work hard, recognise their strengths, and achieve things they didn't think were possible.

Mid-Somerset RTC is a fantastic place for players to develop, with high standards and a strong focus on supporting each player's journey. Zach is proud to be part of an environment that challenges players to be the best they can be, both on and off the pitch.

Safeguarding & Girls

Chris

Safeguarding & Welfare Officer · Head of Girls Centre

Chris plays a vital dual role at Mid-Somerset RTC. As club Safeguarding and Welfare Officer, he ensures every player is in a safe, supportive environment. As Head of the Women and Girls programme, he leads the programme that gives girls across Somerset access to the same high standard of coaching and pathway as the boys.

Head of Football Camps

Ben

Head of Football Camps · U12 White Coach · UEFA B Licence

Ben is a UEFA B Licensed coach with a degree in Sports Coaching and Science, and has been part of Mid-Somerset RTC for over five years. He currently leads football camps and coaches the U12 Whites, having worked across all age groups from 5v5 through to 11v11.

Alongside his work at the club, Ben is part of the JPL All Stars coaching team for the South West. He's spent four summers leading football camps across 25 states in America, been involved in over eight European football tours, and helped lead the Under 8s to back-to-back appearances at the National Championships at St George's Park.

His coaching style is energetic, enthusiastic and positive - setting high standards while creating an environment where players are challenged to improve and always feel supported. He loves seeing players develop through the pathway, build confidence, and create memories that last a lifetime.

Head Coach

Todd

U14–U16 Phase Lead · Head Coach of U16s

Todd has been coaching football for 11 years, with the last four spent at Mid-Somerset RTC. His background is primarily in school PE, and his approach is built around being supportive and positive while creating teams that understand the importance of working together.

He strongly believes that football is a "we game, not a me game" - success comes from players supporting each other, building strong relationships, and putting the team first.

What makes Mid-Somerset special, in Todd's words, is the people - the sense of community, togetherness, and shared ambition throughout the club, on and off the pitch. He's proud of achievements including winning multiple tour tournaments and recently finishing with the joint 3rd best defensive record in the country. Most importantly, he loves seeing players become better teammates, enjoy their football, and develop into confident, committed individuals.

Our Ethos

What We Stand For

Everything we do comes back to three principles.

Welcome & Informed

Every parent and player should feel completely at ease from first contact. We're open, honest, and always happy to answer questions, with no pressure, just clear information whenever you need it.

Development First

Results matter, but development matters more. We measure success by how much each player grows, technically, physically, and as a person, because that's what creates life-changing footballers, not just good ones.

Life-Changing Opportunities

From competing in the Mid Cup in Spain to touring the Netherlands, and representing Mid Somerset on the international stage, we open doors that most academies never could, giving every player experiences that go far beyond the pitch.
