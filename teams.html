Age Groups

Our Teams

Boys U7–U13 and Girls U8–U15 (Girls Centre of Excellence) — with goalkeeper training available at all three venues.

Boys Programme

Boys Teams — U7 to U13

Structured JPL development for boys across all age groups, with sessions at Strode College, Writhlington, and St Mark's.

U7s
Weekday Evenings

Our youngest group — introducing the joy of football in a fun, welcoming environment. Sessions focus on coordination, movement, and basic ball skills to build confidence and a love of the game from the very beginning.

Find Out More
U8s & U9s
Weekday Evenings

Ball mastery and technical foundations. Players develop key movement patterns, 1v1 skills, and a growing understanding of the game through small-sided formats and age-appropriate JPL-aligned sessions.

Find Out More
U10s & U11s
Weekday Evenings

Players begin to develop positional awareness, combination play, and a stronger team shape. Technical work on passing, receiving, and movement off the ball is reinforced in competitive small-sided games.

Find Out More
U12s & U13s
Weekday Evenings

A key phase in the JPL pathway. Training becomes more structured and demanding, with individual development plans, positional specialisation, and the mental side of the game. Players compete in JPL fixtures and representative events.

Find Out More
Girls Centre of Excellence

Girls Teams — U8 to U15

Our dedicated Girls Centre of Excellence provides a specialist JPL development pathway for female players from U8 right through to U15.

U8s & U9s Girls
Foundation Phase
Introducing young female players to the game in a fun, supportive environment. Ball mastery, coordination, and a love of football are the key priorities.
U10s – U12s Girls
Development Phase
Technical and tactical development within the JPL framework. Growing team understanding, positional play, and individual skill in age-appropriate training environments.
U13s – U15s Girls
Performance Phase
High-intensity training mirroring the JPL performance environment. Tactical depth, individual development plans, and pathways to representative and county squads.
Specialist Coaching

Goalkeeper Training

Dedicated goalkeeper development available at all three training venues, for both the Boys and Girls programmes.

Strode College

Street, Somerset — Mon & Fri evenings. Specialist goalkeeper sessions alongside the main group training.

Writhlington Sports Centre

Radstock, Somerset — weekday evenings. High-quality GK coaching for all age groups.

St Mark's School

Bath, Somerset — weekday evenings. Specialist goalkeeper training for boys and girls across all age groups.

The Full Package

What We Offer

Joining Mid-Somerset JPL Talent Centre means more than just training sessions — it is a complete JPL development experience.

JPL-Qualified Coaches
All sessions delivered by qualified coaches with specialist youth development experience in the Junior Premier League framework.
Individual Player Development Plans
Every player receives personalised feedback and a development plan that tracks their progress throughout the season.
JPL Competitive Fixtures
All age groups compete in the Junior Premier League competition structure, providing vital match experience alongside training.
End of Season Awards
We celebrate player progress and achievement each season with a dedicated awards evening for players and families across all age groups.
JPL Pathway Support
We actively support players in progressing through the JPL structure and beyond, including county representative pathways.
Specialist Goalkeeper Coaching
Dedicated GK training available at all three venues for both boys and girls — a rare specialist offering at youth level.
