Age Groups

Our Teams

From our youngest U8s through to our U16s, every age group benefits from age-appropriate coaching and development.

Find Your Group

Age Group Teams

Each group runs its own dedicated training programme tailored to the developmental needs of players at that age.

U8s & U9s
Training: Tuesday evenings

Our youngest age groups focus on the joy of football. Sessions are centred on ball mastery, coordination, and basic movement skills in a fun, low-pressure environment. The emphasis is on engagement and love of the game — developing confidence with the ball and building the foundations for a lifetime in football.

Find Out More
U10s & U11s
📅 Training: Tuesday & Thursday evenings

Players in this group begin to develop a greater understanding of team shape, positional play, and combination football. Technical work on passing, receiving, and movement off the ball is introduced alongside small-sided competitive games to reinforce learning in match-realistic scenarios.

Find Out More
U12s & U13s
📅 Training: Tuesday & Thursday evenings

A key phase in player development. Training becomes more structured and technically demanding, with a focus on individual development plans, positional specialisation, and the mental side of the game. Players in this group regularly compete in the Somerset Development League and county representative fixtures.

Find Out More
U14s & U16s
📅 Training: Tuesday & Thursday evenings

Our oldest age groups are pushed to the highest standard. Training mirrors the intensity and structure of professional academy sessions, with detailed tactical work, video analysis, and performance feedback. Players at this level are actively supported with pathways to professional academies and county representation.

The Full Package

What We Offer

Joining Midsomerset RTC means more than just training sessions — it is a complete development experience.

Qualified FA Coaches
All sessions delivered by UEFA and FA-licensed coaches with specialist youth development experience.
Individual Player Development Plans
Every player receives personalised feedback and a development plan that tracks their progress throughout the season.
Competitive Match Fixtures
All age groups compete in appropriate league and cup competitions, providing vital match experience alongside training.
End of Season Awards Night
We celebrate player progress, commitment, and achievement each season with a dedicated awards evening for players and families.
Academy Pathway Support
We actively support players in pursuing trials at professional academies and help facilitate introductions where appropriate.
Character & Wellbeing Programme
Beyond football, we integrate education around mental health, respect, and positive behaviour throughout our sessions.
