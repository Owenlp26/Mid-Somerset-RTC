Who We Are

About the Academy

Midsomerset RTC is an FA-registered Regional Talent Club based in Somerset, dedicated to identifying and developing talented young footballers aged 8 to 16. As part of the national RTC network, we provide a structured, high-quality pathway that bridges grassroots football and elite academy environments.

Our programme combines technical coaching, physical development, and character education — giving every player the tools they need to fulfil their potential both on and off the pitch. Whether your child is taking their first steps in the game or pushing for a professional pathway, Midsomerset RTC is the place to grow.

What We Do

Our Programme

A three-pillar approach that develops complete footballers, ready for the next level.

Technical Development

Structured sessions focused on ball mastery, technical skill-building, and position-specific training. Our FA-qualified coaches design age-appropriate curricula that challenge every player to improve week on week.

Teamwork & Character

Football is a team sport and life requires character. We embed core values — respect, resilience, determination, and sportsmanship — into everything we do, preparing young people for success on and off the pitch.

Pathways to Progress

Clear development pathways link our youngest players through to senior football. We run trials, liaise with professional academies, and provide every player with transparent feedback on their progression.
