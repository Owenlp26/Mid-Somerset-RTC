What We Do

More than just a football club

Mid-Somerset RTC is Somerset's premier JPL Talent Centre, developing boys and girls aged U7 to U15 into the next generation of footballers.

We do more than coach football: we build confidence, character, and a lifelong love of the game. As part of the official Junior Premier League pathway, our sessions are structured and high quality, but never out of reach, because every child who deserves the opportunity should get it. We combine technical coaching, physical development, and personal growth into one programme unlike anything else in Somerset.

Why Parents Choose Mid-Somerset RTC

A three-word promise we live by every single session.

Approachable

We know how daunting it can be to put your child forward for something new. That's why our coaches and staff are warm, welcoming, and always on hand to help. From your very first enquiry to your child's first session, you'll feel like part of the family from day one.

Affordable

Premium coaching shouldn't come with a premium price tag. We believe every talented child in Somerset deserves access to the best development, regardless of budget. Our programme is priced to be accessible, without ever compromising on quality.

Outstanding

We pride ourselves on developing the next generation of players through academy-level coaching and genuine passion for seeing every child succeed. We've been JPL National Champions and we compete in international tournaments, giving our players life-changing opportunities and real exposure to professional pathways. But what truly sets us apart is our coaches: elite-level professionals with years of experience who are genuinely invested in your child's growth, both as a player and as a person.

Girls Programme

Women and Girls

We built our Women and Girls to give girls the same high level standard of coaching, competition, and opportunity that's too often only available to boys. At St Marks School in Bath, girls from U8 to U15 train at academy level, compete in the Junior Premier League, and travel abroad to represent Mid Somerset on international stages, all while still playing for their grassroots club.

Wherever your daughter is on her journey, whether she's just starting out or ready to push to the next level, there's a proper pathway for her here.
