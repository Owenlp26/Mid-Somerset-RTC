Who We Are

About the Academy

Mid-Somerset JPL Talent Centre is Somerset's premier Junior Premier League Football Academy, dedicated to identifying and developing talented young footballers aged U7 to U15 across Somerset. As part of the official JPL pathway, we provide a structured, high-quality programme that bridges grassroots football and elite academy environments.

Our programme combines technical coaching, physical development, and character education — giving every player the tools they need to fulfil their potential both on and off the pitch. We are proud to offer dedicated provision for both Boys (U7–U13) and Girls (U8–U15) through our Girls Centre of Excellence, alongside specialist goalkeeper training at all three venues.

What We Do

Our Programme

A three-pillar approach that develops complete footballers, ready for the next level.

Technical Development

Structured sessions focused on ball mastery, technical skill-building, and position-specific training. Our JPL-qualified coaches design age-appropriate curricula that challenge every player to improve week on week.

Teamwork & Character

Football is a team sport and life requires character. We embed core values — respect, resilience, determination, and sportsmanship — into everything we do, preparing young people for success on and off the pitch.

JPL Pathway

Clear development pathways within the Junior Premier League. We run trials, support progression through the JPL structure, and provide every player with transparent feedback on their development.
