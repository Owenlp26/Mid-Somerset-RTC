Belgium · 2026

Kempense Meren Cup 2026

In 2026, Mid-Somerset RTC travelled to Belgium to compete in the prestigious Kempense Meren Cup, and came home as champions. Our squad of young players took on clubs from across Europe and came through with medals, memories, and proof of what this programme produces.

Competing internationally isn't just about winning. It's about what players experience, learn, and carry back with them. Our players returned from Belgium with a belief in themselves that no training session alone can build.

Spain · April 2026

MIC Cup 2026

April 2026 saw Mid-Somerset RTC travel to Spain for the MIC Cup, one of the world's most prestigious youth football tournaments. Our squad faced some of the best young clubs from across the globe, including Real Betis Balompié, SC Pro Soccer Academy (USA), and CD Raqui San Isidro from the Canary Islands.

Competing alongside clubs of this calibre, in a professional tournament environment, in a foreign country, is the kind of experience that shapes players for life. It's one of the things that makes Mid-Somerset RTC different.

Italy

Training at the Juventus Academy

One of the most memorable experiences our players have had: a training trip to the Juventus Academy in Italy. Our squad trained on the same hallowed turf used by one of the world's greatest clubs, coached in an environment that few young British players ever get to experience.

Returning from Juventus with medals and a perspective on professional football that no session in Somerset could replicate. These are the moments that last a lifetime.
