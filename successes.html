NATIONAL CHAMPIONS
JPL National Champions 2018 & 2025

JPL National Champions

Two National Championship titles. 2018 proved this club could win at the highest level. 2025 proved it was no fluke. The JPL National Championship is the pinnacle of the Junior Premier League and Mid-Somerset RTC have won it twice.

Be Part of the Next Chapter
Netherlands 2026

Eindhoven Cup 2026

In 2026, Mid-Somerset RTC travelled to the Netherlands for the Eindhoven Cup and didn't just win it once. Four separate age groups returned as champions: U8s, U9s, U10s, and U11s all lifted the trophy on the same trip.

Four trophies from one tournament is the kind of result that speaks for itself. It's evidence of a club that produces winning players at every level, not just in one age group.

U8s Champions U9s Champions U10s Champions U11s Champions
Belgium 2026

Kempense Meren Cup 2026

Our U15s travelled to Belgium for the prestigious Kempense Meren Cup, one of Europe's top youth football tournaments, and came home as champions. Going up against clubs from across the continent, they won the whole thing.

Competing internationally builds more than skill. Our players returned from Belgium with medals, memories, and a belief in themselves that no training session alone can build.

Spain, April 2026

MIC Cup 2026

April 2026 saw Mid-Somerset RTC travel to Spain for the MIC Cup, one of the world's most prestigious youth football tournaments. Our squad faced some of the best young clubs from across the globe, including Real Betis Balompie, SC Pro Soccer Academy (USA), and CD Raqui San Isidro from the Canary Islands.

Competing alongside clubs of this calibre, in a professional tournament environment, in a foreign country, is the kind of experience that shapes players for life.

v Real Betis Balompie v SC Pro Soccer Academy (USA) v CD Raqui, Canary Islands

Coach Successes

Real Madrid, JPL All Stars

Paul at Real Madrid

Paul was at Real Madrid with the JPL Warriors All Stars, not as a spectator or a tourist, but as the coordinator for JPL groups visiting the biggest and most successful football club in the world.

Paul leads the JPL All Stars programmes for both London and the South West. With nearly 10 years of working closely with Real Madrid, if any JPL group in the UK wants to visit, Paul is the person who makes it happen. That is the level of the person standing on the touchline at your child's session every single week.

Category 03

Player Successes

The truest measure of any academy is where its players end up. Players who trained with Mid-Somerset RTC have gone on to sign with professional clubs across the Football League. Boys who came through our programme are now training full-time at some of England's biggest clubs.

This is what the JPL pathway is built for: giving talented young players from Somerset the platform, the coaching, and the exposure they need to reach the professional game. It's happening right now.

The Pathway

Every player at Mid-Somerset RTC is on a real development pathway. We train within the JPL national structure, attend elite international tournaments, and work with coaches who operate at the highest levels of the game.

If your son or daughter has the ability and the work ethic, this programme gives them every opportunity to take it as far as they can.
