Everything you need to know about training at Mid-Somerset RTC: venues, schedules, age groups, and how it all works.

How It Works

A Programme Built Around Your Child

Our sessions are structured, progressive, and designed to make every player better, week after week.

Structured Sessions

Every session follows a JPL-aligned curriculum with warm-up, technical drills, small sided games, and cool down. Age appropriate, progressive, and purposeful.

Expert Coaching

Every session is led by experienced, qualified coaches who make sure each player is seen, challenged, and developed, whatever the size of the group.

Ongoing Development

We track progress and give clear, honest feedback. Parents are kept informed, and every player knows exactly what they're working towards.

What to Expect

From First Session to Unlocking Your True Potential

Every new player starts with a trial session so we can see how they fit and where they're at. You'll meet the coaches, see the setup, and get honest feedback either way, so your child knows exactly where they stand and what to work on next.

Goalkeepers

Specialist Goalkeeper Training

Goalkeeping is one of the most specialist positions in football, yet it's often the most overlooked at grassroots level. At Mid-Somerset RTC, every goalkeeper gets dedicated training at all three venues, led by coaches who understand the unique demands of the position.

Whether your child is a dedicated goalkeeper or an outfield player looking to develop between the sticks, we make sure they get the specialist attention that position deserves.
