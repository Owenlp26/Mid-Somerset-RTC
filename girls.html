Girls Programme

Women and Girls

A dedicated pathway for girls aged U9 to U16 in Bath, built to the exact same standard of coaching, facilities, and ambition as our boys' programme, because girls' football deserves nothing less.

About the Programme

Girls Football Done Right

Our Women and Girls trains every week at St Mark's School in Bath, as part of the official Junior Premier League pathway. Girls here receive academy-level coaching, compete in the JPL, travel to international tournaments, and have genuine pathways to professional football.

Whether your daughter is just starting to fall in love with football or she's ready to push herself to the next level, there's a place for her here in Bath.

Why Choose Us

What Makes Our Girls Programme Different

Official JPL Pathway

Our Women and Girls in Bath is part of the Junior Premier League's official structure, meaning your daughter's development is recognised, tracked, and supported at national level.

Dedicated Coaching

Sessions designed specifically for girls' development at each age group. Our coaches understand what it takes to help girls thrive on the pitch, and they deliver it every week.

Inclusive Environment

With our welcoming, supportive and fun environment, every girl is valued, every voice is heard, and every player is helped to grow. Beyond the football, our girls build a genuine sense of belonging, forming friendships with teammates that last far beyond their playing days and becoming part of a community that feels like family.
